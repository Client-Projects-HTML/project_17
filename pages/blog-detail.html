10 Smart Ways To Store Fresh Vegetables For Maximum Shelf Life

Keeping leafy greens, berries, and root vegetables crisp requires adjusting moisture, temperature, and airflow inside your refrigerator.

1. Keep Herbs in Glass Jars with Water

Treat cilantro, parsley, and asparagus like fresh flowers. Trim the stems and stand them upright in an inch of fresh water.

2. Wrap Leafy Greens in Cloth Towels

Excess moisture is the primary cause of wilted spinach and kale. Place a dry paper towel inside plastic containers to absorb moisture droplets.
